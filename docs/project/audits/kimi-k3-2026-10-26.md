1. **Fireworks (user-held + $25 offer) — red-team attack generation, Suite A.** `tools/eval.ts --redteam 50 --rule <R> --model <fireworks-id>` × 11 rules × 3 models (e.g. Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct, Llama-4-Scout, a small 8B for contrast) × 3 reps. Produces: per-rule catch-rate table with Wilson CIs — the first honest recall numbers (my SEC-01 TOKEN probe predicts measurable misses on SEC-01/SEC-03).
2. **Modal ($30 confirmed) — bulk repetition + corpus v2 folding.** Same suites ×5 reps for variance, plus auto-fold caught/missed variants into `corpus.json` v2 (needs issue #7 fixed first: `--out` NDJSON). ≈ 5M tokens ≈ **$5–12**. Produces: regression deltas baseline-v1→v2 and an expanded held-out corpus judges can inspect.
3. **Baseten ($100 + 2M TPM, when claimed) — nightly full-matrix runs during the window.** All rules × all models × N=50 × 5 reps ≈ 25M tokens ≈ **$25–40**, leaving headroom. Produces: trend table across the week — "unsafe-action rate vs day" is a strong judge artifact.
