The Problem

Vibe-coding right now is like giving a Ferrari to a teenager with no brakes.

AI coding agents (Cursor, Codex, OpenCode, Hermes) are miraculous. A non-technical creator can type "build me a marketplace for dog walkers" and get real code in minutes. But under the hood, three catastrophic things happen every day:

💣

The Invisible Blast Radius

The AI accidentally deletes a database table, exposes private API keys to the public internet, or installs an unverified package that steals passwords.

😴

"Are You Sure?" Fatigue

Harnesses interrupt you 50 times with technical dialogs. Non-technical users suffer from 93% habituation: you just click "Yes" blindly without reading until disaster strikes.

📜

The "Sticky Note" Illusion

Developers think putting rules in a markdown file (AGENTS.md) keeps them safe. When the AI gets confused or context is long, it ignores the note completely.

The Solution

The 4 Pillars of vibe-fence

How vibe-fence makes vibe-coding completely safe, educational, and fun.

1

The Bouncer at the Door (Hooks)

Instead of hoping the AI follows polite text rules, vibe-fence stations an unbypassable programmatic guard (PreToolUse hooks) between the AI and your computer. If the AI tries to run rm -rf or push secrets to GitHub, the bouncer blocks the execution before it even starts.

💡 Plain English: A real steel deadbolt on your front door instead of a "Please don't break in" sticky note.
2

The Magic Undo Time Machine

Rather than stopping you to ask technical permission for every harmless change, vibe-fence lets the AI fly in Turbo Mode. Before any big action, it silently takes a microscopic snapshot of your project. If you dislike what the AI did, one click or typing undo restores your project immediately.

💡 Plain English: Driving at top speed with Formula-1 safety nets, instant rollback, and airbags.
3

The Teaching Dial (Sweller's Law)

Beginners need encouraging explanations; experienced creators want fast execution without chatter. The Teaching Dial (Max → Med → Min → Silent) shifts the companion persona smoothly from an empathetic teacher who explains concepts simply, to a silent executioner who stays out of your way.

💡 Plain English: Training wheels that lift off gently as you learn to balance, without you falling off.
4

One Single Rulebook (Universal Sync)

Whether you open Cursor today, OpenCode tomorrow, or Claude Code on your laptop, you only define your project's identity once in .fence/app-profile.json + src/framework/context-budget.yml via tools/generate.ts. vibe-fence keeps Cursor/OpenCode/Claude settings in sync.

💡 Plain English: Universal adapter plug so your safety works on any tool anywhere in the world.
