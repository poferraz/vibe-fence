# vibe-teach — explain the work

Use plain language to say what changed, why it matters, and how the result was checked. Describe uncertainty and limitations. When a choice is needed, present concrete options and trade-offs appropriate to the user; do not claim a choice is required when the user already authorized the work.

For a finding, use the fixed summary and project-relative location from the local report. Do not repeat a secret or assume a check proves a project safe. An optional model explanation is advisory and must not change the deterministic result.

Cards, automatic teaching, instrumentation, and verification ladders are design ideas, not established runtime behavior. Do not claim they ran unless their output is observed. Follow the user's instructions and the project's actual verification commands.
